Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the coaching inquiry form:

  • who you are (parent / guardian, player, coach / team representative, or other)
  • the player's name and age
  • the training interest and development goals you select
  • your name and email address, your phone number if you add it, and any message you write

If you're sending an inquiry about a player who is under 18, please make sure you're their parent or guardian, or have their parent's or guardian's permission.

How it's used

Your details are used only to reply to your baseball coaching inquiry. They are not sold or shared for marketing. Sending an inquiry does not book a session.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to Coach Hans.

Fonts, images and scripts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, photography from the Pexels image service and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks. These services receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files. Training selections you make on the page may be kept in your browser for the rest of your visit so the form can be pre-filled; they are not sent anywhere until you submit the form.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, send a note through the inquiry form and mention it in your message.
