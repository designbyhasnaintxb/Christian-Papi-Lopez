Meet Papi

Born from a spirited conversation.

Introducing VamosPickle, born from a spirited conversation between Christian “Papi” Lopez and his two racquet-sport-loving daughters. Inspired by their passion for Pickleball, we set out to democratize high-level training for Youth and Adult players.

Recognizing a gap in quality instruction at the foundational level, we envisioned a platform offering meticulously curated classes and events, fostering confidence and improvement.

How Vamos trains

Not random drills.
A training system.

Papi built VamosPickle’s curriculum from 30+ years across five racquet sports. His progression is clear in his own words: the feet first, then the strokes, then the mind — with a whole-racquet-sport perspective behind all of it.

  1. 01

    Footwork first

    Everything starts with the feet: split steps, and tracking movement in every direction. Papi has been blunt about weak footwork in youth tennis and pickleball — so it’s where his camps begin.

    Split stepMovement in every direction

  2. 02

    Stroke technique

    Then the ground game — forehands, backhands, drives and dinks. Then volleys and overheads, with the emphasis on body position and placement.

    ForehandBackhandDriveDinkVolleyOverhead

  3. 03

    The mental approach

    Papi calls mindset the most important element. Players who aren’t sure they belong get a simple answer to “can I play?” — Vamos. Confidence is trained, not assumed.

    ConfidenceMindset“Vamos”

  4. 04

    A racquet-sport perspective

    Tennis, platform tennis, badminton, padel, pickleball. Papi’s view: the sports are different, each has its merits, and players should learn from each other. The goal is simple — learn to play pickleball at a higher level.

    TennisPlatform tennisBadmintonPadelPickleball
