08 · Approach

Coaching approach

A simple approach. Player first.

A general outline of how coaching can work — always shaped around the individual player.

  1. Step 01

    Understand the player

    Start with the player: where their game is today and what they enjoy about it.

  2. Step 02

    Identify areas to develop

    Agree on the areas that matter most right now.

  3. Step 03

    Train with purpose

    Work on those areas with clear, practical focus.

  4. Step 04

    Build confidence

    Help the player feel more prepared and comfortable in their game.

  5. Step 05

    Keep developing

    Revisit, adjust and keep building from there.
